Who We Are

Made in our own unit

{{ site.company_name }} manufactures stainless steel houseware at its own manufacturing unit in Delhi. Our products are made in-house by skilled workers using good quality material, so the finish and consistency stay the same order after order.

We make containers and bowls in a range of shapes, sizes and capacities, which lets our buyers stock a full range from a single supplier.

How We Supply

Built for bulk buyers

Sold by weight

Orders are supplied by weight rather than by piece — commonly in 20 kg, 50 kg and 100 kg lots — which keeps bulk pricing simple.

Competitive pricing

Because we manufacture in-house, we are able to offer pricing that compares well with the market for the same quality.

Pan India supply

We supply wholesalers, distributors and resellers across India with reliable logistics partners.

Wholesale only

We work with businesses on bulk orders. We do not sell single pieces to retail customers.
