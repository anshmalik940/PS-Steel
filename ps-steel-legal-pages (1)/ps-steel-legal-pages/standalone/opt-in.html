1. What This Page Covers

This page explains how PS Steel may contact you for business updates and marketing, what you agree to when you give consent, and how to stop messages at any time. It works together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

2. What You Agree To

When you tick the consent box on our enquiry form, submit details through one of our social media lead forms with a consent statement, or clearly message us asking to receive updates, you allow PS Steel to contact you on the mobile/WhatsApp number and email you provided with:

  • Product catalogues, new arrivals, stock and price updates;
  • Bulk-order schemes, discounts and business offers;
  • Reminders and follow-ups about your enquiry or order;
  • Festive greetings and important business announcements.

4. Service Messages (Not Marketing)

Even if you do not opt in to marketing, we may still contact you about your own enquiry or order, for example to reply to your question, share a quotation you asked for, confirm an order, or tell you about pickup and payment. These are not promotional messages.

5. Consent Is Voluntary

Marketing consent is not required to send an enquiry, get a quotation or buy from us. You can leave the box unticked. Saying no will never affect how we treat your enquiry or order.

6. How We Record Your Consent

We record that you gave consent, along with the date and time and the form or channel you used, so we can show you when and how you agreed. Your consent applies only to PS Steel and the purposes listed above, and we will not pass your number to others for their own marketing.

7. How to Withdraw Consent (Opt Out)

You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it:

  • Reply STOP to any WhatsApp or SMS message from us;
  • Message us on WhatsApp at +91 9810122370 saying "STOP" or "Unsubscribe";
  • Email [OFFICIAL EMAIL] with the subject "Unsubscribe";
  • Click the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

We will stop marketing messages within [7] days (usually sooner). Withdrawing consent does not affect messages already sent, or service messages about an existing order.

8. Frequency, Costs and DND

Message frequency depends on our offers and updates, and we aim to avoid unnecessary or excessive messages. Standard message, call or data charges from your operator may apply. We follow applicable Indian rules on commercial communication, including your DND (Do Not Disturb) preferences where they apply, and WhatsApp's business messaging policies.

9. Your Data

Your contact details are used only as described in our Privacy Policy. You can also ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time.
