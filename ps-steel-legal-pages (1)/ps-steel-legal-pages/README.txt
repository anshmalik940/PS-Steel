PS STEEL - LEGAL PAGES
======================
Folder contents
- standalone/  : full ready pages (own header, CSS, dark mode) - privacy-policy.html, terms.html, opt-in.html
- fragments/   : only the content block + legal-styles.css, for pasting into your existing site template
- consent-checkbox-snippet.html : opt-in checkbox for the Bulk Enquiry form

How to use
1. Pick ONE style: standalone pages, or fragments inside your site template.
2. URLs to use: /privacy-policy/ , /terms/ , /opt-in/  (opt-in is a NEW page - add it to your routes).
3. Add the consent checkbox to the enquiry form (keep it unticked by default; save yes/no + timestamp).
4. Add "Communication Consent" link in the footer next to Privacy Policy and Terms.

Fill these yellow placeholders (search for '[' in the files)
- [GST NUMBER]      : your GST number
- [OFFICIAL EMAIL]  : official business email
- [PERSON NAME] / [Owner / Proprietor] : grievance contact (Privacy Policy, last section)
- [7]  : days (quotation validity, claim window, opt-out time)
- [24] / [12] / [30] : retention months / log months / response days (Privacy Policy)
