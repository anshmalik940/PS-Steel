1. Scope and Who We Are

PS Steel ("we", "us", "our") is a trader and supplier of stainless steel houseware (containers, bowls and related products) at A-92, Wazirpur Industrial Area, Delhi, India, GST No. [GST NUMBER]. This policy applies to ps-steel-blush.vercel.app (the "Website"), our enquiry forms, WhatsApp/phone/email communication with you, and our social media advertisements and lead forms. It explains what data we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and your rights.

For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), PS Steel acts as the Data Fiduciary for the personal data you give us.

3. Why We Use Your Information

  • Responding to you: answering enquiries, sending quotations, product details and pricing.
  • Orders and accounts: confirming orders, preparing goods, invoicing, GST and accounting records.
  • Marketing (only with consent): WhatsApp, SMS, email or call updates about products, schemes and offers. See our Communication Consent page.
  • Advertising and analytics: measuring which ads and pages work, and showing relevant ads.
  • Security and fraud prevention: protecting the Website and our business from spam and misuse.
  • Legal compliance: tax, accounting, and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

4. Consent and Legal Basis

We process your data based on your consent (for example when you submit an enquiry or tick the marketing consent box), for the purpose you approached us for (answering your enquiry and completing your order), and where required to comply with law. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 9); this will not affect processing done before withdrawal or processing we must continue by law, such as tax records.

5. Who We Share Data With

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • Service providers: website hosting, messaging (WhatsApp/SMS/email tools), analytics and IT support, bound to use data only for our services.
  • Advertising platforms: Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google/YouTube and similar platforms, through pixels, tags and lead forms (see section 6).
  • Professional advisers: accountants, CA and legal advisers, where necessary.
  • Authorities: government bodies, courts or law enforcement when legally required.
  • Business transfer: a successor, if the business is ever sold or restructured, subject to this policy.

6. Cookies, Pixels and Social Media Advertising

We run advertisements on social media and online platforms. To measure and improve them, we and these platforms may use cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies on the Website.

  • Essential cookies keep the Website working.
  • Analytics cookies tell us how visitors use the Website.
  • Advertising cookies/pixels measure ad performance, and may build audiences so relevant ads are shown to you elsewhere.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and control ad personalisation in your Meta, Google and other account ad settings. If you submit details through a social media lead form, that platform's own privacy policy also applies to that data.

7. How Long We Keep Data

  • Enquiry data that did not turn into an order: up to [24] months, unless you ask us to delete it earlier.
  • Order, invoice and GST records: for the period required by tax and accounting laws.
  • Marketing contact details: until you withdraw consent or ask us to stop.
  • Website logs and analytics: typically up to [12] months.

After this, we delete or anonymise the data.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards: HTTPS on the Website, restricted access to enquiry data, and secure hosting. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affecting you occurs, we will take reasonable steps to notify you and the authorities as required by law.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Under the DPDP Act and applicable law, you can:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase your data, where we no longer need it for a legal or business purpose;
  • Withdraw consent for marketing or any other processing, as easily as you gave it;
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of your death or incapacity;
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any right, contact us using the details below. We may ask you to verify your identity, and we aim to respond within [30] days.

10. Children

The Website is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Data Location and Third-Party Services

Some of our service providers (hosting, analytics, ad platforms) may store or process data on servers outside India. We use providers that apply reasonable security standards. Links on the Website to other sites (e.g. WhatsApp, social media) are governed by those sites' own policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version, with its "Last updated" date, will always be available at this page. Please review it periodically.
